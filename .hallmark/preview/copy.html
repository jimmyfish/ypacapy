20 strings from src/ and src-tauri/ that read unclear or used two names for one thing, all now changed in the code. Each one is shown in the style it renders with: the old text on the left, the new text on the right in a blue frame.

The scan found no words from the banned list, no em dashes, no puffery, no binary contrasts and no emoji. Most of the copy is already plain and concrete, so the fixes are small.
